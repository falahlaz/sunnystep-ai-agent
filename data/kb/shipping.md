# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery times
Orders within Singapore arrive in 2 to 4 business days. Orders to Malaysia arrive in 5 to 8 business days. Orders are dispatched within 1 business day of payment.

## Shipping fees
Shipping is free for orders of SGD 80 or more. Orders below SGD 80 pay a flat SGD 6 shipping fee.

## Tracking a parcel
Once an order ships, the carrier name and tracking number are available on the order. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Lost or delayed parcels
If a parcel has not arrived 7 business days after the estimated delivery date, our customer care team opens an investigation with the carrier. The assistant cannot declare a parcel lost or issue a replacement.
