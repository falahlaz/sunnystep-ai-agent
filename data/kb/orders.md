# Orders & Payment

## Changing or cancelling an order
An order can be changed or cancelled only before it ships. Changes and cancellations are handled by the customer care team, not by the assistant.

## Changing the delivery address
For security, delivery addresses are never changed through chat. The customer care team verifies identity before changing an address.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayNow and GrabPay. Payment details are never requested in chat.
