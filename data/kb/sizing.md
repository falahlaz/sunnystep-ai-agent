# Sizing & Exchanges

## Size guide
All shoes use EU sizing from 35 to 46. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size. Measure your foot length in the evening, when feet are slightly larger.

## Wide feet
The Cloudwalk and Harbor lines come in a wide fit. Other models run true to standard width.

## Size exchanges
A different size of the same model can be exchanged free of charge within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock. Exchanges are arranged by the customer care team.
