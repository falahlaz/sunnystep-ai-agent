# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Unworn shoes in their original box can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days are counted from the day the carrier marks the parcel as delivered.

## Comfort guarantee
If a pair feels uncomfortable after light indoor wear, it can be returned once per customer within 30 days of delivery, even if lightly worn. Shoes worn outdoors or showing sole wear are not eligible.

## Final sale items
Items marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged, except for manufacturing defects covered by the warranty.

## How to start a return
Returns are approved by our customer care team. The assistant collects the order number and reason, then hands the request to a team member who emails a prepaid return label within 1 business day.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the returned pair arrives at our warehouse. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item was defective.
