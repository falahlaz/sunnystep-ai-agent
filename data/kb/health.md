# Comfort & Foot Health

## Foot conditions and medical advice
Our shoes are designed for everyday comfort but they are not medical devices. We cannot advise whether a shoe will treat or relieve a medical condition such as plantar fasciitis, bunions or diabetic foot problems. Please consult a podiatrist or doctor.

## Comfort features
Most models have a cushioned midsole, a removable insole and a flexible knit upper. Product pages list the features of each model.
